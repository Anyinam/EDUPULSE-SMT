# EduPulse School Implementation Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains, in school-facing language, what to expect when your school is being set up on EduPulse. It mirrors our internal implementation process so there is clarity on responsibilities, timing, and success milestones.

## What EduPulse Will Help You Set Up

EduPulse helps your school run academic operations, reporting, payment oversight, and subscription control in one place. During implementation, our goal is to move your school from account creation to confident daily use.

## What We Need From Your School

Before full setup, please provide the following:

1. Authorized school signatory or proprietor contact
2. Billing contact name, phone number, and email address
3. School logo and preferred brand color
4. Student, staff, or class data files if you want us to assist with import
5. Preferred dates and times for admin training

## Implementation Phases

### 1. Discovery And Setup Confirmation

We confirm:

- Your school size and operating model
- Your main users: proprietor, headmaster, teachers, finance team
- Your selected plan: `Free`, `Basic`, or `Premium`
- Your target go-live date

### 2. Account Creation

We create:

- Your school organization in EduPulse
- Your school code
- Your first proprietor login
- Your initial tenant and security configuration

If your school chooses a paid plan, payment must be confirmed before the plan is considered active.

### 3. Data Import And Configuration

If you want help importing data, we will:

- Review your files
- Flag any data issues before import
- Import approved records into your school account
- Verify sample records with you after import

If your files need cleanup first, we will tell you exactly what to correct.

### 4. Kickoff Training

We run a guided training session for your leadership and school operations contacts. The session typically covers:

- Logging in and user roles
- Adding teachers and students
- Attendance workflow
- Report and approval workflow
- Payment and billing visibility
- Where to get support

### 5. First-Week Activation

During your first week, we expect to help your school complete these milestones:

1. First successful login
2. First teacher added
3. First students added
4. First attendance recorded
5. First finance or payment workflow confirmed

This is the point where implementation becomes real operational use.

## Expected Timeline

The exact timeline depends on how ready your school data and decision makers are, but a normal rollout looks like this:

1. Day 1: information collection and account setup
2. Day 2-3: payment confirmation for paid plans, data validation, kickoff scheduling
3. Day 3-5: import, training, and first live usage
4. Day 5-7: follow-up review and activation confirmation

## Your Responsibilities

Your school helps implementation succeed by:

- Providing accurate data files
- Assigning one responsible contact person
- Ensuring the proprietor or authorized decision maker is available when needed
- Attending the kickoff session
- Completing first-week operational actions in the platform

## Billing During Implementation

- `Free` plan schools can begin using the platform immediately after setup.
- `Basic` and `Premium` plan schools are activated after payment confirmation.
- If your school downgrades later, already-paid access remains active until the end of the paid billing period.
- If student volume changes, billing follows the agreed plan rules and active student counts.

## Support During Go-Live

If you need help during implementation or after go-live:

1. Use the Help and Support area in the platform where available
2. Reply to your onboarding email thread
3. Share the exact issue, affected user, and screenshots if possible

We use ticket-based support so your issue is tracked and not lost.

## Implementation And Support Service Levels

The timelines below are operating targets for a normal implementation. They depend on complete school-side inputs and timely responses.

### Implementation Milestones

| Milestone | Target Service Level | Dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial onboarding response | Within 1 business day | School inquiry received with valid contact details |
| Account setup after required inputs are complete | Within 1 business day | Required setup details received |
| Data import review | Within 2 business days | Import files received in reviewable format |
| Kickoff training scheduling | Within 2 business days | School shares availability |
| First follow-up after kickoff | Within 3-7 calendar days | Kickoff session completed |

### Support Response Targets

| Issue Level | Example | Target First Response | Target Update Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Login outage, billing integrity concern, school fully blocked | Within 4 business hours | Same business day until stable |
| High | Major workflow blocked with no workaround | Within 1 business day | Daily until resolved or workaround provided |
| Normal | Standard usage issue, configuration help, training request | Within 2 business days | Every 2 business days if still open |
| Low | Minor question, cosmetic issue, enhancement suggestion | Within 3 business days | As scheduled by support queue |

### What Can Delay These Timelines

- Missing data files or incomplete setup information
- Unconfirmed payment for paid plans
- Delayed feedback from the school on import exceptions
- Unavailable decision makers for approvals or training
- Issues that require deeper technical investigation

## How We Define A Successful Implementation

Your implementation is considered successful when:

- Your proprietor can log in
- Your school data is available or intentionally deferred
- Your team has completed training
- Your users can perform the core workflows needed for daily operations
- There is no unresolved blocker preventing normal use

## Common Reasons Implementation Slows Down

The most common delays are:

- Missing or incomplete school data
- Delayed payment confirmation for paid plans
- Unavailable decision makers
- Unclear ownership on the school side
- Training sessions missed or postponed

If any of these happen, we will tell you early and reset the next milestone clearly.

## After Go-Live

After implementation, EduPulse continues with:

- First-week follow-up
- Ongoing support
- Billing and renewal guidance
- Upgrade or expansion support as your school grows

## Contact Path

For onboarding and implementation questions, reply through your existing EduPulse onboarding thread or your assigned support contact.
