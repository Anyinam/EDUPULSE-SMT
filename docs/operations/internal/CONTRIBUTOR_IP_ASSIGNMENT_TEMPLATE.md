# EduPulse SMT Contributor IP Assignment Template

## Purpose

This is a draft internal template for assigning intellectual-property rights in work created for EduPulse SMT. It is a practical drafting aid for counsel, not signed legal advice.

## Draft Template

**Contributor Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement**

This Contributor Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement is made on [Date] between:

1. **EduPulse SMT / [Registered Entity Name]**, of [Address] (the "Company")
2. **[Contributor Full Name]**, of [Address] (the "Contributor")

## 1. Purpose

The Contributor will create, assist with, improve, review, or otherwise contribute to software, documentation, workflow materials, technical designs, prompts, templates, policies, or other materials related to EduPulse SMT.

## 2. Definitions

For this agreement:

- **Work Product** means all software code, source files, scripts, database models, APIs, technical designs, prompts, documents, templates, playbooks, specifications, business methods, visual materials, communications materials, and derivative works created, authored, modified, or contributed by the Contributor in connection with EduPulse SMT.
- **Intellectual Property Rights** means all copyright, database rights, trade-secret rights, confidential know-how, design rights, trademark rights, patent rights where applicable, and any similar rights recognized under applicable law.

## 3. Assignment Of Rights

The Contributor irrevocably assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee, all present and future right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product and all associated Intellectual Property Rights, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

This assignment applies to all Work Product created:

1. During the engagement
2. For the benefit of EduPulse SMT
3. Using Company information, systems, repositories, documentation, or instructions
4. As improvements, derivative works, adaptations, or refinements of existing EduPulse SMT materials

## 4. Further Assurances

The Contributor agrees to sign further documents and do all reasonable acts necessary to confirm, perfect, register, or evidence the Company's ownership of the Work Product and related rights.

## 5. Pre-Existing Materials

The Contributor must disclose in writing any pre-existing materials they intend to use. Unless explicitly listed in a signed schedule, all materials delivered in connection with EduPulse SMT will be treated as fully assigned to the Company under this agreement.

## 6. Moral Rights And Similar Rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor waives and agrees not to assert moral rights or similar personal rights in the Work Product against the Company, its successors, licensees, customers, or assigns.

## 7. Confidential Information

The Contributor acknowledges that EduPulse SMT involves confidential business and technical information, including payment-routing logic, reconciliation logic, cryptographic controls, school operations workflows, launch materials, incident templates, onboarding playbooks, and security architecture.

The Contributor agrees:

1. To keep that information confidential
2. Not to use it except for authorized work
3. Not to disclose it to any third party without written approval

## 8. No Conflicting Rights

The Contributor represents that:

1. They have authority to enter this agreement
2. Their contributions do not knowingly infringe third-party rights
3. They are not under any conflicting obligation that would prevent assignment under this agreement

## 9. Return And Deletion

Upon request or termination of the engagement, the Contributor will return or permanently delete Company confidential information and work materials in their possession, subject to legal retention obligations if any.

## 10. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Ghana, unless counsel determines another governing-law structure is more appropriate for the final signed version.
