# EduPulse SMT Lawyer Document Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist tells you exactly what to send your lawyer for:

1. Business incorporation
2. Intellectual-property and confidentiality structuring
3. Ghana data-protection and data-processing application work

It separates repository documents that already exist from external founder/company documents you still need to gather.

11. `docs/operations/internal/STANDARD_OPERATING_PROCEDURES.md`

### Founder And Corporate Documents To Gather

1. Proposed company name options in order of preference
2. Founder full legal names, national IDs/passports, phone numbers, email addresses, and residential addresses
3. Proposed directors, shareholders, secretary, and beneficial owners
4. Intended shareholding structure and initial share capital
5. Registered office address and principal business address
6. Short company objects or business-activity description
7. Brand assets you want protected now: name, logo, product names, taglines, domain names
8. Any existing founder, contractor, or collaborator agreements already signed
9. Any evidence showing when the software, brand, and documentation were created

### What To Ask Counsel To Deliver

1. Incorporation advice and filing package
2. Founder/shareholder structuring advice
3. IP ownership confirmation and assignment strategy
4. Execution-ready contractor, employee, and advisor IP/confidentiality documents
5. Trademark or brand-protection advice where appropriate

## Packet 2: Data Protection and Data Processing Application

### Repository Documents To Send

1. `LEGAL_COUNSEL_PACKET.md`
2. `LAWYER_EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.md`
3. `GHANA_DATA_PROTECTION_TECHNICAL_BRIEF.md`
4. `compliance/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
5. `compliance/DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md`
6. `compliance/DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`
7. `compliance/INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PLAN.md`
8. `compliance/INFORMATION_SECURITY_POLICY.md`
9. `compliance/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`
10. `docs/operations/internal/STANDARD_OPERATING_PROCEDURES.md`
11. `AUDIT_INTEGRITY_SYSTEM.md`
12. `SECURITY.md`

### Supporting Operational Information To Gather

1. Current hosting stack and data-storage locations
2. Current or expected subprocessors: hosting, email, payment, SMS, analytics, backup
3. Internal privacy contact and incident-response contact details
4. Registered address and phone number for the legal entity once incorporated
5. Data categories processed: student, parent, staff, school admin, billing, admissions, report-card images, exam-result data
6. Retention periods you intend to apply in production
7. Data subject request intake channel and response workflow
8. Cross-border transfer details if any provider stores or supports data outside Ghana

### What To Ask Counsel To Deliver

1. Review of controller/processor positioning between EduPulse SMT and each school
2. Advice on whether and how to register with the Ghana Data Protection Commission
3. Review of privacy policy, DPA, retention, incident, and security wording
4. Final contract language for school onboarding and data-processing terms
5. Advice on admissions, report-card OCR, and external exam-result processing under Ghana law
