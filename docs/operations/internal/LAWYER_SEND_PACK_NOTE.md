## Short Message To Lawyer

Please review EduPulse SMT for:

1. business incorporation and founder/shareholder structuring,
2. IP ownership and confidentiality protection,
3. contractor and contributor assignment language, and
4. the correct legal framing of the software, workflows, and supporting business materials.

The platform is a Ghana-focused multi-tenant school SaaS product with strong emphasis on payment controls, reconciliation, audit integrity, role-gated workflows, and data-protection-sensitive school operations.

## Founder Information To Send Alongside The Docs

Send these non-repo items with the packet:

1. proposed company name options,
2. founder full legal names and ID details,
3. proposed directors, shareholders, and beneficial owners,
4. intended shareholding structure and initial share capital,
5. registered office and business address,
6. business activity description, and
7. any signed founder, contractor, or collaborator agreements already in existence.
