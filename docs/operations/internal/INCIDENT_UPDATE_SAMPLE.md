# EduPulse SMT Incident Update Sample

## Purpose

This is a filled sample showing how EduPulse SMT should document an operational incident internally and communicate updates to a school.

## Internal Incident Update

- Incident title: Bright Future Academy finance workflow verification delay
- Incident ID: INC-2026-03-24-001
- Status: `Monitoring`
- Severity: `Normal`
- Incident commander: Daniel Asare Anyinam
- Last updated: March 24, 2026 12:10 GMT

### Summary

During live validation, Bright Future Academy's bursar could not immediately confirm the expected payment-recording workflow because one fee item was mapped to the wrong category.

### Impact

- Affected schools: Bright Future Academy only
- Affected workflows: Finance workflow validation, payment categorization review
- Data integrity risk: `No`
- Billing integrity risk: `No`

### Actions Taken

1. Reproduced the bursar workflow with the school billing contact on a live call.
2. Confirmed the issue was configuration-related and not a payment verification failure.
3. Updated the fee category mapping and re-ran the workflow successfully.

### Current Blocker

- School is confirming the corrected mapping aligns with internal bursar expectations.

### Next Step

- Owner: Daniel Asare Anyinam
- ETA: March 24, 2026 15:00 GMT
- Next update time: March 24, 2026 16:00 GMT
