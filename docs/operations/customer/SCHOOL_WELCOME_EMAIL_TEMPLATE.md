## What We Need From Your School

To begin setup smoothly, please share the following:

1. Authorized proprietor or signatory contact
2. Billing contact name, phone number, and email address
3. School logo and preferred brand color
4. Student, staff, or class data files if you want import support
5. Your preferred dates and times for kickoff training

## What EduPulse SMT Will Do

Once the required inputs are complete, we will:

1. Create your school organization and proprietor access
2. Confirm your plan and activation path
3. Review and import approved data where needed
4. Schedule and run kickoff training
5. Guide your school through first live workflows
6. Follow up after go-live to remove blockers

## Implementation Timeline

Our standard operating targets for a normal rollout are:

- Initial onboarding response: within 1 business day
- Account setup after complete inputs: within 1 business day
- Data import review: within 2 business days
- Kickoff scheduling: within 2 business days
- First follow-up after kickoff: within 3-7 calendar days

## Billing Expectations

- `Free` plan schools can begin using the platform after setup is complete.
- If your school is on `Basic` or `Premium`, activation happens after payment confirmation.
- If your school later downgrades, already-paid access remains active until the end of the current paid billing period.

## First-Week Success Milestones

We aim to help your school complete these actions in the first week:

1. First successful proprietor login
2. Teachers added
3. Students added or imported
4. Attendance recorded
5. At least one finance or payment-related workflow confirmed

## How Support Works

If you need help during setup or after go-live:

1. Use the Help and Support section in the platform where available
2. Reply to this email thread
3. Share the issue, affected user, and screenshots where possible

### Support Response Targets

- Critical issue: within 4 business hours
- High issue: within 1 business day
- Normal issue: within 2 business days
- Low issue: within 3 business days

## Common Causes Of Delay

The most common rollout delays are:

- Missing or incomplete data files
- Unconfirmed payment for paid plans
- Delayed approvals or responses from school owners
- Missed training sessions

If any of these happen, we will reset the next milestone clearly with your team.
