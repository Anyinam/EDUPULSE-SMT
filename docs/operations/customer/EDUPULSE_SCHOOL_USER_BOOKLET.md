# EduPulse SMT School User Booklet

## Welcome

EduPulse SMT is a school operations platform that brings academic work, reporting, payment visibility, and leadership oversight into one system.

This booklet is a short introduction for schools that are starting with EduPulse SMT. It is designed for proprietors, headmasters, administrators, finance staff, and teachers.

## What EduPulse SMT Helps Your School Do

EduPulse SMT helps schools run important daily and termly work in one place.

Core use areas include:

- Student and staff records
- Attendance tracking
- Report card and terminal report workflows
- Headmaster and proprietor approvals
- Payment recording and finance visibility
- Subscription and billing visibility

The main benefit is clarity. Your school can work in one structured system instead of depending on scattered files, messages, and manual follow-up.

## Who Uses The Platform

### Proprietor

The proprietor uses EduPulse SMT to review school performance, monitor billing visibility, and maintain leadership oversight.

### Headmaster

The headmaster uses EduPulse SMT to supervise academic workflows, review reports, and support operational follow-through.

### Teachers And Homeroom Teachers

Teachers use EduPulse SMT for class and student workflows, especially around academic records and reporting.

### Finance Staff

Finance users work with payment records, transaction visibility, and reconciliation support.

### Admin And Operations Staff

Admin users support setup, data entry, user management, and day-to-day platform coordination.

## What Setup Usually Looks Like

Most school rollouts follow this order:

1. School details are confirmed.
2. The school account is created.
3. Proprietor access is issued.
4. Student, staff, or class data is added or imported.
5. Key users are trained.
6. The school begins live use.

If your school is on a paid plan, activation happens after payment confirmation.

## What Your School Should Prepare

Before go-live, your school should be ready with:

1. A main school-side contact person
2. Proprietor or authorized signatory details
3. Billing contact details
4. Staff, student, or class data if import support is needed
5. A preferred kickoff or training date

Strong preparation shortens implementation time and reduces confusion in the first week.

## The First Things Your School Should Do

Your first priority is successful access and role setup.

The school should confirm:

1. The proprietor can log in successfully.
2. The headmaster or lead admin can log in successfully.
3. The correct users have the correct roles.

After that, the school should begin with these core workflows:

1. Add or verify student and staff records
2. Start attendance or daily school operations
3. Review report workflow responsibilities
4. Review finance and billing visibility with the proprietor

## How Schools Usually Use EduPulse During A Term

EduPulse SMT is best used as a term-based operating system.

During a normal term, schools typically use it for:

1. Daily and weekly school operations
2. Mid-term academic follow-up
3. Report preparation and approvals
4. End-of-term review
5. Next-term readiness and renewal follow-up

Completed terms should remain part of the school's historical record.

## End Of Term And Billing

Schools should understand one important distinction:

- Term closure is an academic and operational event.
- Subscription renewal is a billing event.

These are connected, but they are not the same thing.

In practical terms:

- Closing a term should not erase old records.
- Historical term records should remain available.
- The next term may trigger renewal or billing actions depending on the school plan.
- If billing is overdue, some access may become restricted until payment is resolved.

## What Schools Should Know About Access

EduPulse SMT uses plan and billing status to control parts of the platform.

Schools should know the basics:

- `Free` plan schools use the platform within free-plan limits.
- `Basic` and `Premium` schools use the platform according to their paid-plan rules.
- If payment becomes overdue, access may move through grace, overdue, and suspension states.
- Historical records remain important for recovery and school continuity.

## What Success Looks Like In The First 30 Days

The first month is going well when:

1. Leadership understands the platform's purpose.
2. Key users can log in without friction.
3. Roles are set correctly.
4. Student and staff data is usable.
5. At least one real academic workflow is running.
6. The proprietor understands billing visibility.
7. The school knows how to request support.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

Schools get better results when they avoid these mistakes:

1. Delaying ownership on the school side
2. Adding users without clear role decisions
3. Importing data without checking sample records afterward
4. Waiting until term end to start using the platform properly
5. Ignoring billing visibility until access becomes urgent

## Recommended Internal Rollout Order

For the smoothest introduction, use this order:

1. Proprietor orientation
2. Headmaster and admin orientation
3. Finance orientation if needed
4. Teacher or homeroom orientation
5. First live workflow in the platform

## How To Ask For Support

When your school needs help, include:

1. The name of the school
2. The name and role of the affected user
3. A short description of the issue
4. A screenshot where possible
5. Whether the issue is blocking live work

This helps EduPulse SMT resolve issues faster.
