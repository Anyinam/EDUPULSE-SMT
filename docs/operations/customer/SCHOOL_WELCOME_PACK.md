## What Your School Should Prepare

Please share the following so implementation can begin smoothly:

1. Authorized proprietor or signatory contact
2. Billing contact name, phone number, and email address
3. School logo and preferred brand color
4. Student, staff, or class data files if import help is required
5. Preferred dates and times for kickoff training

## What EduPulse SMT Will Do

Once the required inputs are complete, we will:

1. Create your school organization and initial proprietor access
2. Confirm your plan and activation path
3. Review and import approved school data where needed
4. Run kickoff training for your leadership and operations team
5. Guide your school through first live workflows
6. Follow up after go-live to remove blockers

## Your Implementation Journey

### Phase 1: Setup Confirmation

We confirm your school size, key users, selected plan, and target go-live date.

### Phase 2: Account Creation

We create your school account, school code, and first proprietor login.

If your school is on a paid plan, activation happens after payment confirmation.

### Phase 3: Data Import

If you want import support, we review your files, flag issues early, and verify sample records with you after import.

### Phase 4: Kickoff Training

We train your team on:

- Logging in and roles
- Adding teachers and students
- Attendance workflow
- Report workflow
- Payment and billing visibility
- How to get support

### Phase 5: First-Week Success

Your first week should include:

1. First proprietor login completed
2. Teachers added
3. Students added or imported
4. Attendance recorded
5. At least one finance or payment-related workflow confirmed

## First-Week Checklist

- [ ] Proprietor has logged in successfully
- [ ] Headmaster or admin has logged in successfully
- [ ] Teachers have been added
- [ ] Students have been added or imported
- [ ] Attendance has been recorded
- [ ] Branding and report settings have been reviewed
- [ ] Billing visibility has been explained to the proprietor
- [ ] School knows where to request support

## Billing Expectations

- `Free` plan schools can begin using the platform after setup is complete.
- `Basic` and `Premium` plans are activated after payment confirmation.
- If your school later downgrades, paid access remains active until the end of the current paid billing period.
- Billing decisions follow the agreed plan rules and active student counts.

## What Can Slow Down Implementation

The most common causes of delay are:

- Missing or incomplete data files
- Unconfirmed payment for paid plans
- Delayed school feedback on data issues
- Unavailable decision makers
- Missed training sessions

If any of these happen, we will reset the next milestone clearly with your team.

## How Support Works

If you need help:

1. Use the Help and Support section in the platform where available
2. Reply to your onboarding email thread
3. Share the issue, affected user, and screenshots where possible

We handle support through tracked tickets so requests are not lost.

## What Success Looks Like

Implementation is considered successful when:

- Your proprietor can log in
- Your key users have completed training
- Your school can perform core daily workflows
- There is no unresolved blocker preventing normal use
