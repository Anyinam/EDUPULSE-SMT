UPGRADE TRIGGERS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Free Tier → Basic Plan
└─ Hit 50-student capacity limit
└─ Need advanced reports & analytics
└─ Want full financial tracking
└─ Need enhanced DSAR support (Standard SLA)

Basic Plan → Premium Plan
└─ Need to exceed 200 students
└─ Want AI risk prediction
└─ Want automated payment processing
└─ Want AI-generated exam questions
└─ Need enhanced audit logging & compliance support
└─ Require webhook signature verification

REVENUE PROJECTIONS (Per 100 Schools)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Scenario: 70% Basic / 30% Premium Mix (Average: 100 students/school for both)

Basic Plan (70 schools × avg 100 students):
  70 schools × [(GH₵360 × 3) + (70 students × GH₵12 × 3)]
  = 70 schools × GH₵3,600/year = GH₵252,000/year

Premium Plan (30 schools × avg 100 students):
  30 schools × [(GH₵750 × 3) + (70 students × GH₵22 × 3)]
  = 30 schools × GH₵6,870/year = GH₵206,100/year

Total Annual Revenue (100 schools): GH₵458,100

Average ARR per School: GH₵4,581
