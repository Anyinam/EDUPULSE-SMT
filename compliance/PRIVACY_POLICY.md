# Privacy Policy (Ghana - Act 843)

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EduPulse Limited ("EduPulse", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when schools, staff, students, and guardians use the EduPulse platform (the "Service").

This policy is designed to comply with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

When a school completes registration on EduPulse, that registration action confirms acceptance of this Privacy Policy and the Data Retention and Deletion Policy as part of onboarding.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect data necessary to provide the Service, including:

- School and account data: school name, school ID, contact details, subscription plan.
- Staff data: name, email, phone, role, login credentials.
- Student data: name, student ID, class, academic records, attendance.
- Parent/guardian data: name, phone, email (if provided by the school).
- Usage data: login events, device info, IP address, audit logs.
- Payment data: transaction references, invoices. We do not store full card details. Payments are processed by Paystack.

## 3. Legal Basis and Purpose

We process personal data to:

- Provide the Service (lesson notes, grading, reporting, communication).
- Maintain security and prevent fraud.
- Provide support and system notifications.
- Meet legal and regulatory requirements.
- Manage subscriptions, billing, and receipts.

For student data, the school is the primary data controller and EduPulse acts as a data processor on the school's behalf.

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

- Authorized school staff using the platform.
- Service providers (hosting, email, payment processing) under strict contracts.
- Regulators or law enforcement if legally required.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain data as needed to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations. See our Data Retention Policy for details.

## 6. Security Measures

We use industry-standard controls including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS)
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Audit logging
- Regular backups

## 7. Data Subject Rights (Act 843)

Individuals have rights to:

- Access their data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion (subject to legal obligations)
- Object to certain processing

Requests can be sent to privacy@edupulse.com.gh. We will respond within the statutory period.

## 8. Children and Minors

EduPulse is used by schools. Student data is processed under the school's authority and for educational purposes only.

## 9. Cross-Border Transfers

If data is stored or processed outside Ghana, we ensure adequate protections and contractual safeguards.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated to schools.
