# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Effective Date: May 31, 2026

This DPA forms part of the agreement between the school ("Controller") and EduPulse SMT or its duly incorporated operating entity ("Processor").

## 1. Roles

- Controller: The school determines purposes and means of processing student and staff data.
- Processor: EduPulse SMT processes data only on documented instructions and only to provide the contracted school platform services.

## 2. Scope and Purpose

EduPulse SMT processes data to provide the Service, including school operations, admissions workflows, reporting, payment support, document parsing, academic analytics, and related support services.

Where the Controller has accepted the relevant onboarding notice and the processing is otherwise lawful, EduPulse SMT may also de-identify Controller-supplied academic and operational records and use the resulting non-personal aggregated data for benchmarking, educational trend analysis, curriculum research, system optimization, and related analytics services.

## 3. Data Categories

- Student data: name, ID, class, academic records.
- Staff data: name, email, phone, role.
- Guardian data (if provided): name, phone, email.
- Admissions data: application details, supporting documents, and school review status.
- Academic document data: report-card files, parsed fields, confidence flags, and related review actions.
- Examination data: school-uploaded WAEC or BECE result records and related academic analysis outputs.
- Usage data: logins, audit logs.
- De-identification outputs: anonymized statistical records, broad cohort tags, benchmark summaries, and aggregated analytical datasets generated from Controller data after identifiers are removed.

## 4. Controller Responsibilities

- The Controller is responsible for determining the lawful basis for the personal data it uploads or instructs EduPulse SMT to process.
- The Controller must ensure it has authority to upload student, guardian, admissions, report-card, and examination data.
- The Controller remains responsible for notices, consents, and internal approvals required by applicable law.
- The Controller is responsible for deciding whether its lawful basis and notices are sufficient to permit the de-identification and aggregation described in the contract documents.

## 5. Aggregated Data and Educational Research Insights

### Authorization for De-Identification

The Controller explicitly authorizes EduPulse SMT to de-identify and permanently anonymize student academic performance records, including mock examination scores, BECE results, related cohort performance data, and associated institutional metadata where the processing is otherwise lawful and covered by the onboarding notice and contract documents.

### Irreversible Anonymization

EduPulse SMT will apply technical and organizational measures designed to strip direct personal identifiers and direct school identifiers before source data enters the shared analytics layer. Those measures are intended to remove names, student identification numbers, contact details, and direct school names so that the resulting dataset is treated as non-personal data and is not reasonably traceable to an individual student or specific institution.

### Permitted Use Cases

Once data has been irreversibly anonymized, EduPulse SMT may process the resulting aggregated dataset to generate:

- national, regional, and district-level educational performance trends and benchmarks;
- curriculum assessment insights, including Chief Examiner historical report mapping and subject-gap analysis;
- statistical research reports, white papers, and learning-outcome studies for Ghanaian education; and
- benchmark dashboards and system-optimization analytics for subscribing schools.

EduPulse SMT will use reasonable measures to keep shared outputs in aggregated statistical form and to keep the analytics environment logically separated from the live operational environment where practicable.

### Data Ownership

The Controller retains ownership of its raw, identifiable operational records. Subject to applicable law and contract, EduPulse SMT retains ownership of the resulting aggregated, anonymized macro-statistical datasets, benchmark models, visual metrics, and related analytical outputs generated from irreversibly de-identified platform data.

## 6. Security Measures

EduPulse SMT implements:

- Access control and least privilege
- TLS encryption in transit
- Tenant isolation and role-based access controls
- Backups and monitoring
- Incident response procedures
- Audit logging for sensitive operational and financial actions

## 7. Subprocessors

EduPulse SMT may use subprocessors for hosting, email, payment processing, backup, and related infrastructure. A current list is available upon request or by contract schedule.

## 8. Data Subject Rights

EduPulse SMT will provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in responding to data subject requests and other privacy-related obligations.

## 9. Breach Notification

EduPulse SMT will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller data.

## 10. Return or Deletion

Upon termination, EduPulse SMT will return or delete personal data as instructed, subject to legal obligations and documented retention requirements.

## 11. Audit and Information Rights

EduPulse SMT will provide reasonable information to demonstrate compliance, subject to confidentiality, security, and proportionality safeguards.

## 12. Cross-Border Processing

Where Controller data is stored or accessed outside Ghana, EduPulse SMT will use contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

## 13. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by Ghanaian law.
