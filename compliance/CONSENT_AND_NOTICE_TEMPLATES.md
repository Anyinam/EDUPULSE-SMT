# EduPulse SMT Consent And Notice Templates

Effective Date: May 31, 2026

This document provides working draft language for EduPulse SMT customer onboarding, parent admissions, and legacy-record migration. It is not legal advice. Your lawyer should review and adapt the templates before production use.

## 1. School Registration Notice

Recommended placement:
Registration page, school onboarding agreement, and signed commercial paperwork.

Suggested wording:

By registering a school account on EduPulse SMT, the school confirms that:

1. the school is acting as the primary data controller for student, guardian, staff, academic, attendance, admissions, and fee data entered into the platform;
2. EduPulse SMT is appointed as a data processor or service provider for the limited purpose of hosting, securing, organizing, and processing that data on the school's instructions;
3. the school has the legal authority to upload and manage the records it places in the system;
4. the school will provide any privacy notices, parent communications, or consent forms required under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), education-sector obligations, or its own school policies;
5. where the school uploads WAEC or BECE result lists, continuous-assessment marks, or pre-existing historical records, it has confirmed a lawful basis for that processing; and
6. the school understands that EduPulse SMT may irreversibly de-identify and aggregate lawfully supplied academic and operational records for benchmarking, national or regional educational trend analysis, curriculum research, system optimization, and related educational insights, without identifying any student or named school in the shared analytics outputs; and
7. the school accepts EduPulse SMT's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Data Retention Policy, and Data Processing terms.

Optional short checkbox label:

I confirm that my school has authority to manage student records in EduPulse SMT and will provide any notices or consents required by law.

Recommended explicit analytics checkbox label for registration:

I agree to the Data Processing Agreement, including EduPulse SMT's use of anonymized, aggregated academic metrics for educational benchmarking, research, and system optimization.

Recommended tooltip or explainer text:

We remove student names, IDs, contact details, and direct school identifiers before approved data enters EduPulse SMT's shared analytics layer. Benchmarking and research outputs are presented only in aggregated form and are designed not to identify your students or your school.

## 2. Parent Admission Privacy Notice And Consent

Recommended placement:
Parent admissions page, admissions packet, or admission-account activation flow.

Suggested wording:

To process this admission request, the school and EduPulse SMT will collect and use the learner's profile details, guardian contact details, supporting documents, and related admissions information.

This information will be used only for:

1. admissions screening and school follow-up;
2. review of supporting academic or identity documents;
3. communication with the parent or guardian about admission decisions, correction requests, tests, school visits, or enrollment next steps; and
4. lawful educational administration after admission where applicable.

If previous report cards, examination records, or similar documents are uploaded, the school may analyze them to assess the learner's academic readiness and placement needs.

Suggested checkbox label:

I confirm that I am the parent or lawful guardian of this learner and I consent to the school and EduPulse SMT using the submitted information and documents for admissions review and related school communication.

Optional BECE-specific extension where relevant:

Where the learner's BECE or other examination records are later provided to the school, I understand that the school may use those records for placement, admissions review, academic support, and internal school performance analysis.

## 3. Existing Student Data Migration Consent

Recommended placement:
Printed form, PDF circular, school app notice, or signed digital migration campaign for students already enrolled before the school's EduPulse SMT rollout.

Suggested wording:

Subject: Notice Of Student Record Migration To EduPulse SMT

Dear Parent or Guardian,

[School Name] is implementing EduPulse SMT as its secure school management platform. To improve academic administration, fee visibility, reporting, parent communication, and student support, the school plans to migrate existing student and guardian records into the system.

The records to be migrated may include:

1. student identity and class information;
2. parent or guardian contact details;
3. attendance and academic history;
4. fee balances and payment records; and
5. other school records reasonably necessary for administration and student support.

The school will use this data only for legitimate educational and administrative purposes. EduPulse SMT will host and protect the records on the school's behalf.

Suggested consent statement:

I confirm that I am the parent or lawful guardian of the student named below and I consent to [School Name] migrating my ward's existing school records into EduPulse SMT for school administration, academic tracking, billing, communication, and student support.

Parent or Guardian Name: ____________________

Student Name: ____________________

Signature: ____________________

Date: ____________________

## 4. BECE Or WAEC Analytics Notice

Recommended placement:
School policy pack, internal school privacy notice, or a separate addendum when the school uses EduPulse SMT for school-level external examination analytics.

Suggested wording:

Where [School Name] uploads WAEC or BECE result lists into EduPulse SMT, the data will be used only for:

1. student placement, transition, or academic support;
2. subject and cohort trend analysis;
3. school performance reviews across academic years;
4. internal leadership reporting; and
5. comparison against lawfully available regional or national aggregate statistics.

The school will not publish identifiable student examination results through EduPulse SMT to unauthorized third parties.

Where the school participates in EduPulse SMT benchmarking or macro-analytics, shared outputs should remain anonymized and aggregated. Public or customer-facing reports should avoid small groups or narrow slices that could reveal a specific student or school.

Suggested parent acknowledgement clause:

I understand that [School Name] may use my ward's examination results within EduPulse SMT for internal academic analysis, student support, and school performance monitoring.

Optional school addendum for anonymized platform benchmarking:

I understand that after direct identifiers are removed, EduPulse SMT may combine examination and academic statistics from multiple schools to produce anonymized benchmarking and educational trend reports that do not name my ward or the school.

## 5. Counsel Review Checklist

Before production use, ask counsel to confirm:

1. the controller-processor split is described correctly in your contracts;
2. the school's chosen legal basis is appropriate for new admissions, legacy student migration, and external exam analytics;
3. the wording matches Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and your final company registration details;
4. any WAEC-facing integration or data-sharing language is approved before it appears in customer materials; and
5. anonymization claims are technically supportable and match the real ETL or analytics architecture; and
6. the final notices match the actual product behavior and record-keeping process.
