Define the steps for handling security incidents and data breaches.

## 3. Severity Levels

- Low: No data impact, minor issue.
- Medium: Limited data exposure, contained.
- High: Unauthorized access to personal data.
- Critical: Large-scale breach or service disruption.

## 4. Response Steps

1. Detect and verify
2. Contain and isolate
3. Eradicate root cause
4. Recover services
5. Notify affected parties
6. Post-incident review

## 5. Notification

- Notify schools without undue delay.
- Notify Data Protection Commission as required by law.

## 6. Evidence and Logging

- Preserve logs, system snapshots, and audit trails.
- Maintain chain-of-custody.

## 7. Post-Incident Review

- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions
- Update policies and controls
