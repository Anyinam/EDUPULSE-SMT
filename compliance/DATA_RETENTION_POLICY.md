# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Define how long EduPulse retains data and how it is deleted.

## 2. Retention Periods

- Student academic records: retained for duration of school contract + 5 years.
- Staff records: duration of contract + 3 years.
- Audit logs: 2 years.
- Billing records: 7 years (tax compliance).

## 3. Deletion and Anonymization

- Data is securely deleted after retention period.
- If deletion is not possible, data is anonymized.

## 4. Legal Holds

Data may be retained longer if required by law or legal proceedings.

## 5. Customer Requests

Schools may request deletion or export upon contract end, subject to legal obligations.
